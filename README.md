# 🧟‍♂️ Relaxed Zombie Shooter

> *"They're slow. They're harmless. They can't reach you. Take your time."*

Welcome to the **Relaxed Zombie Shooter**, the chillest apocalypse you'll ever experience. Perched high in your invincible watchtower, you overlook a procedural rural town overrun by the undead. There's no rush, no health bar, and no pressure. Just you, your rifle, and an endless supply of bullets.

## 🎮 Features

-   **Procedural Rural Town**: Every round generates a new layout with a strict grid system, ensuring organized town centers and sprawling farm outskirts.
-   **Strict Grid Zoning**: Houses, shops, barns, and silos are placed with purpose. Fences define property lines, and roads connect the chaos.
-   **X-Ray Vision**: Zombies think they can hide behind buildings? Think again. Our advanced optics reveal them as silhouettes even when occluded.
-   **Nav Markers**: Lost track of the last walker? After 5 minutes, our tactical system highlights off-screen zombies for you.
-   **Dynamic Atmosphere**: Day/Night cycles (per round), diverse weather, and trees that sway in the wind.
-   **Chill Vibes**: Toggle the lo-fi beats with a single keypress.

## 🛠️ Technical Details

Built with **Vanilla JavaScript** and **HTML5 Canvas**.
-   **3D Projection**: Custom 2.5D rendering engine projecting 3D coordinates onto a 2D canvas.
-   **Depth Sorting**: robust z-sorting ensures zombies walk *behind* fences but *in front* of the spooky forest.
-   **Performance**: Optimized rendering with segmented road quads to prevent visual glitches at extreme ranges.
